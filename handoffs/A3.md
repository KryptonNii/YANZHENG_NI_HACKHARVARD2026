## Done
- `forecast.py`: Poisson–gamma recruitment (Anisimov & Fedorov, Stat Med 2007;26(27):4958–75).
  Exact \(M^{-1}\). Milestones, duration, enrollment curve, dropout inflation,
  `update_with_actuals` (conjugate Gamma; sites without data stay on the prior predictive),
  `estimate_rate_hyperparameters`. `ForecastResult.duration_draws` is what A2 reads directly.
- `supply.py`: weekly per-site inventory, FIFO expiry, lead time, IRT projection that does not
  know future dropouts, emergency shipments on stockout. Strategies differ only in the initial
  allocation and share one resupply rule. `compare_strategies` uses the same patients.
  Per-draw conservation: produced = dispensed + expired + destroyed at close.
- This pass kept that simulation. It pre-groups dispensing events by week and scans a boolean
  expiry mask instead of comparing the threshold row. Same seed and inputs match the previous
  per-draw arrays (40-site upfront/on-demand, short shelf life, posterior update, BOREAS both
  strategies).
- Shipment `MetricRange`s use unit `"count"` through normal validation (contract v4). See
  [A3_CONTRACT_CHANGES.md](A3_CONTRACT_CHANGES.md). No `model_construct` bypass remains.
- Curve bands use the first **500** of the requested forecast draws; milestones use all of them.
  Supply asks for **500** (`SUPPLY_DRAWS`) and may run fewer under the existing work budget
  (floor 100). Both pairs are on the result. The caps were not lowered to chase 300 ms.
