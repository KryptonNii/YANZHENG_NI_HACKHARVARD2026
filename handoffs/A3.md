# A3 — Forecasting and drug supply · HANDOFF

Branch `dev` (shared folder). An earlier commit on `agent/forecast` (tp-forecast worktree) is obsolete — ignore it.

## Done
- `engine/trialpilot_engine/forecast.py`: Poisson–gamma recruitment (Anisimov & Fedorov, Stat Med 2007;26(27):4958–75,
  doi:10.1002/sim.2956). Exact time-to-n via M⁻¹(Gamma(n,1)), vectorised. Milestones FPI/LPI/LPLV/DB lock,
  duration, enrollment curve, dropout inflation, `update_with_actuals` (conjugate Gamma per site; sites without
  data drawn from the prior predictive, never zero), `estimate_rate_hyperparameters`. `ForecastResult.duration_draws`
  is used directly by A2's engine.
- `engine/trialpilot_engine/supply.py`: weekly per-site inventory, FIFO expiry, lead time, IRT projection that does
  not know future dropouts, emergency shipments on stockout. Strategies differ only in the initial allocation; they
  share one resupply rule. `compare_strategies()` runs both on the same patients.
- Run: `uv run --project engine pytest engine/tests/test_forecast.py engine/tests/test_supply.py`
  → **21 + 16 tests**, all passing except the perf test (see risks). `ruff` clean. mypy --strict NOT yet run.
- Calibration: built-in defaults put upfront waste P50 at ~53% (40 sites), against the industry median of 50% for
  IMP kits (IQVIA 2024, "Reducing Waste with Intelligent Automation", Duerr) and the 60% lost reported by ZS (2025,
  "Rethinking waste management in clinical supply chains"). On-demand: ~3% (40 sites) to 10–22% (real 138–334-site
  trials, mostly leftover safety stock).

## Demo notes
- Flip "on-demand supply": waste drops from ~60% to ~16% on BOREAS, but shipments roughly double. That's the honest
  trade-off. Stockout risk is shown, with a warning above the threshold.
- Excluding sites lengthens enrollment and duration. Home/remote visits change nothing unless the user enters a sourced
  uplift (shown as an explicit assumption).
