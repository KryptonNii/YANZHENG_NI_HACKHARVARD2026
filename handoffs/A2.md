## Worked example (TEST fixture values — not real data; full table in docs/model.md §8)
1 site, 10 patients, 12 months, 3 visits (frozen + ambient blood draws, 2 dispensing visits):
patient time = 10 × 3 × 20 km ÷ 30 km/h + 10 × 3 × 2 h = **80 h**; site staff = 1,700 min ÷ 60 + 6 monitoring
visits × 4 h + 10 h queries = **62.33 h**; kits = 20 needed ÷ (1 − 0.5) = 40; totals **$334,240 / 3,570.2 kg CO2e**.
Remote monitoring 50 % → trips 6 → 3, staff 56.33 h; V2 at home → patient time 63.3 h but cost goes up (honest trade-off).
